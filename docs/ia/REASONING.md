# IA reasoning (not for the page)

Agent-only. Do not paste this into landings, metadata, or CTAs. The contract is [`../IA.md`](../IA.md).

---

## How this was decided

Two lenses on the same copy:

1. **Marketing** — beachhead naming, conversion, don’t list vaporware platforms.
2. **Readers** — a GroupMe pickleball organizer (I’m-in rec night) and a tennis club volunteer (Facebook / WhatsApp / email, not GroupMe).

They agreed on terminology and hub-vs-spoke split. They split on how much to say about a quiet side thread.

---

## GroupMe vs other chats

GroupMe is the word that makes pickleball organizers nod. It also makes a tennis volunteer bounce if it is the *identity* of the product.

**Working rule:** Name GroupMe where it is the beachhead (pickleball; hub Why-it-works; hub subhead). Keep the *headline* generic. Tennis stays “club chat.” One short “other group chats later” on the hub — not WhatsApp/Slack/Discord as a feature list (reads like fake parity).

Hub CTA is **Bring your group** so tennis-shaped visitors are not sent away at the button. Pickleball CTA stays GroupMe-specific.

---

## “Open play”

Wrong for these nights. Organized rec with an I’m-in / RSVP list is not DUPR drop-in. Readers say **rec night**, **Tuesday night**, **I’m in**. Tennis says **round robin / ladder / club night**.

Landing copy must not use “open play” for these events. (The word can exist later if we ever market true drop-in.)

---

## Spreadsheet vs “software project”

“Software project” was clever to the owner and one pickleball reader (fear of becoming IT). Most readers don’t go there. **Spreadsheet** (pickleball) and **clipboard / draw sheet** (tennis) are the pains they already have.

**Owner lock (2026-09-15): spreadsheet is fine.**  
Default pickleball hero metaphor: *without turning Tuesday night into a spreadsheet project.*  
Do not use “software project” on the landings.

---

## Quiet parallel GroupMe

**Operations (true, not for the hero):** Harvest who’s-in from the social group; post back occasionally; run the bot in a **parallel GroupMe** so command chatter doesn’t mute the main thread.

**Marketing:** Don’t explain the architecture. **Do** avoid “same GroupMe, nothing changes” if setup will ask for a second group — readers called that a bait-and-switch.

**Compromise in IA.md:** one line — *We keep bot chatter out of the main thread.* Details belong in onboarding, not H1.

This also means hub FAB “one night, one truth” should not imply a single chat transcript. The *night* is one source of truth; the *threads* may be two.
